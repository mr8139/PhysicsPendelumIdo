# User Guide: PhysicsPendelumIdo

This application is designed to study the physics of a pendulum, perform simulations, and conduct real-world measurements using your smartphone's sensors.

## Main Sections

### 1. Registration and Login
When you first launch the app, you need to register or log in. Most features are only available to authorized users.
- **Registration**: Enter a unique user code and a date.
- **Login**: Use your credentials to access the protected features.

### 2. Pendulum Simulation (Animation)
In this section, you can interact with a visual model of a mathematical pendulum.
- Use the **slider (SeekBar)** at the bottom of the screen to change the length of the pendulum string.
- The app automatically calculates the oscillation period (T) using the formula $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$ and displays it on the screen.

### 3. Sensor Measurement (Sensors)
This tool allows you to measure the period of a real pendulum using your phone's accelerometer.
- **How to perform a measurement**:
    1. Securely attach the phone to the pendulum bob (mass).
    2. Tap the **"Start Measurement"** button.
    3. A 10-second countdown will begin, accompanied by beeps. Use this time to start the pendulum swinging.
    4. After the countdown, recording starts automatically. The app will capture 5 full oscillation periods.
    5. A double beep will sound when the measurement is complete.
- **Results**: The app calculates the period (T), frequency (f), and estimated string length (L).
- **Saving**: You can save the measurement to your history by clicking **"Save Measurement"**.

### 4. Calculator and Formulas
- **Calculations**: Manually enter parameters to calculate various pendulum characteristics.
- **Formulas**: A reference guide for the basic physical formulas used in the app.

### 5. Graphics and History
- **Graphics**: Visualize real-time acceleration data.
- **History**: View all previously saved measurements.

### 6. Educational Content
- **Videos**: This section contains helpful videos regarding the physics of oscillations.

## Navigation Menu
A popup menu is available on the main screen, providing the following options:
- **Info**: Information about the developer and the application.
- **Back**: Navigate to the previous screen.
- **Logout**: Sign out of the current account.
- **Exit**: Close the application.
